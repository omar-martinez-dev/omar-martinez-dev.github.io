02 · How I build

Product judgment belongs in the architecture.

  1. 01

    Start with the user’s interruption.

    I look for the moment a workflow breaks, then reduce the product to the smallest experience that removes that friction.

  2. 02

    Make ownership explicit.

    State, storage, system frameworks, and SDKs get visible boundaries so behavior stays predictable and testable.

  3. 03

    Treat trust as product quality.

    Privacy explanations, accessibility, failure states, and monetization are designed with the same care as the primary interaction.
